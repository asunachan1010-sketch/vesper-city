SYSTEM / 01

The Technology

Vesper possesses technology generations ahead of its public infrastructure. Much of it survives because the city continually repairs, modifies and repurposes machines that should have been obsolete decades ago.

Archive 002-C / Biological Record

The Altered.

Vesper does not officially have superhumans. It has accidents, patients, experiments and people who survived things they should not have survived.

The term “Altered” is informal. Different institutions use different terminology, often depending on who is paying for the research.

Some cases can be traced to Saint Dorian. Others appear to have unrelated origins. Some are the result of deliberate experimentation. Others were never supposed to happen.

The government denies the existence of a unified program connecting these cases.

Archive 002-D / Political Network

The Vesper Circle.

The Circle is not a government. It does not need to be.

It is a network of old families, institutional allies, political figures, corporate interests and people who owe one another more than money.

Its power comes from accumulated obligations: favors, marriages, secrets, political support, financial relationships and information.

The Circle rarely acts directly. It places people in rooms and waits for the right person to make the right decision.

Archive 002-E / Power Structures

Two systems
built to coexist.

Law & Government

Vesper's institutions remain functional on paper. Courts process cases. Police respond to calls. Elections occur. Offices change hands.

Beneath the surface, however, political influence reaches deep into appointments, investigations, contracts and priorities.
