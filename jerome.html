There is no verifiable record of Jérôme Veyl before the age of nineteen.

02 / HISTORY

A history
that begins at nineteen.

The earliest confirmed evidence connected to Jérôme Veyl appears when he is approximately nineteen years old. Even then, the available records are fragmented.

Small robberies, fires, sabotage and unauthorized intrusions were attributed to an unidentified young man during the following years. Several investigations were abandoned for lack of evidence. Others were never formally connected.

What changed was the scale.

Bank robberies, attacks on police facilities, industrial raids and public acts of destruction eventually made "Voile" impossible for Vesper authorities to treat as a collection of unrelated incidents.

04 / INCIDENT

The University
Incident.

Vesper University

During a national congress hosted at Vesper University, Jérôme Veyl attempted to seize control of the campus. The rector was taken hostage and hundreds of students were placed under threat.

Veyl's stated objective was to force the Nocturne to reveal his identity publicly. If the demand was not met, students were to be killed one by one during a live broadcast.

The operation was interrupted before the takeover could be completed. Veyl was captured by the Nocturne with assistance from VCPD officer Hector and transferred to Blackthorn Institute.

The official report considers the incident a failed hostage operation.

Unofficially, The Veil has never completely accepted that explanation.

07 / BLACKTHORN

The patient
inside the institution.

Jérôme Veyl is currently held at Blackthorn Institute, a maximum-security psychiatric facility located in The Veil.

His movements are restricted and his interactions are monitored. He is considered a high-risk offender and is subject to enhanced security procedures.

Despite the restrictions, Veyl has become familiar with the institution's routines, staff and security procedures. He appears particularly attentive to changes in behavior among the people who work around him.

Whether that attention represents deliberate study, ordinary curiosity or something else remains undetermined.
