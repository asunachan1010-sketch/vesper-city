FILE 001 / VEYL, JÉRÔME

Jérôme
Veyl

VOILE

There is no verifiable record of Jérôme Veyl before the age of nineteen. No childhood. No family. No school records. Nothing that explains where he came from.

What exists instead is a growing trail of crimes, fires, disappearances and public incidents that eventually earned him a name of his own. Vesper City calls him Voile. Blackthorn calls him a high-risk offender.

The distinction has never seemed particularly important to Jérôme.
