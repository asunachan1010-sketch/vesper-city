Archive 001 / The City

Yesterday
lives here.

Vesper is a city built from conflicting eras. Ancient stone sits beneath experimental steel. Machines designed decades ago still operate alongside technologies that officially should not exist.

The future did arrive in Vesper. It simply arrived badly.

Generations of ambitious engineers, industrialists and scientists attempted to turn the city into a model of tomorrow. Their machines survived longer than their companies, their governments and sometimes their creators.

Nothing was ever truly discarded. Old infrastructure was repaired. New systems were built on top of older ones. Entire districts became layered records of technological ambition and urban decay.

“Nothing in Vesper is ever truly obsolete.”
