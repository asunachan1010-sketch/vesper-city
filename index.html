The City

Vesper was built for the future. The future simply failed to arrive the way its architects promised. Now the city survives through old machines, impossible technologies and generations of repairs.

Vesper's technology was designed for a different century. Some of it survived. Some of it evolved. Most of it was simply repaired.

The result is a city where advanced artificial intelligence can operate inside a machine that looks forty years old, where experimental medicine exists beneath abandoned factories, and where no one can quite agree on when the future actually happened.
